# RunTracker

A timer for speedrunning Wii games, which splits your run into sections and compares each one against your best.

Speedrunning means finishing a video game, or part of one, as fast as possible. RunTracker is not a jogging app: it is the stopwatch a speedrunner keeps open on a second screen. It has the routes for New Super Mario Bros. Wii, Mario Kart Wii, and Mario Party 9 built in. You press a key at the end of each level, track, or board, and it shows right away whether you are ahead of or behind your personal best.

It is a small project used by a handful of players. A few routes may still change as they are checked against official speedrun records.

## What you can do with it

- Pick a game and a category (about 35 routes in total) and time a run section by section.
- See each section turn green or red depending on whether you are ahead of or behind your best, with gold for a new best section.
- Try the "auto-split" feature (still in testing), which listens to the game's sound and splits automatically when it hears the level-complete music.
- Choose your own keys for split, undo, skip, pause, and reset.
- Save your runs, review your stats, and fix a mistimed split afterward.
- Compare your best times with other players on a shared leaderboard.
