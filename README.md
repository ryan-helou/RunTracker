Speedrunning means finishing a video game, or part of one, as fast as possible. RunTracker is the stopwatch a speedrunner keeps open on a second screen. It has the routes for New Super Mario Bros. Wii, Mario Kart Wii, and Mario Party 9 built in. You press a key at the end of each level, track, or board, and it shows right away whether you are ahead of or behind your personal best.

## What you can do with it

- Pick a game and a category (34 routes in total) and time a run section by section.
- See each section turn green or red depending on whether you are ahead of or behind your best, with gold for a new best section.
- Try the "auto-split" feature, which listens to the game's sound and splits automatically when it hears the level-complete music.
- Choose your own keys for split, undo, skip, pause, and reset.
- Save your runs, review your stats, and fix a mistimed split afterward.
- Compare your best times with other players on a shared leaderboard.
